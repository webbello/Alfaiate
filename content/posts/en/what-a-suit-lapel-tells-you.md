If you want to judge a jacket quickly, look at the lapel. It is shaped by hand and iron, so it shows how much care went into the whole coat.

## Does it lie flat?

A good lapel curves gently and lies against the chest. If it lifts away or ripples, the inside has not been shaped well.

## Look at the stitching

The stitches around the edge should be even and small. A buttonhole on the lapel, even a real one that is never used, is a sign of attention to detail.

## Feel the roll

Hold the lapel between your fingers and let it fall. It should roll softly, not fold sharply like paper.

None of this means a plain jacket is a bad one. It is simply a quick way to compare two jackets that cost the same.
