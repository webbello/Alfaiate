A first fitting goes faster when you arrive prepared. Bring these things and the tailor can start measuring straight away.

- **The shoes you will wear with the clothes.** Trouser and skirt lengths change with the heel.
- **A garment that fits you well.** It gives the tailor a real example to compare with.
- **Pictures or a sketch** of what you want, even a rough one from a phone.
- **Your fabric**, if you already have it. If not, ask the tailor how much to buy before you shop.

## Wear the right layers

Wear the kind of clothes you will wear underneath the finished garment. A shirt fitted over a thick jumper will not sit right on a hot day.

## Ask before you leave

Ask when the next fitting is, what the tailor needs from you before then, and how you will agree the price. Write it down, or send it to yourself in a message, so nothing is forgotten.
