A garment that looks good in the shop can be miserable in the heat. Before you buy, check the cloth itself.

## Light, and able to breathe

Cotton and linen let air through. Hold the cloth up to the light: if you can see the shape of your hand through it, it will usually feel cooler.

## Scrunch it

Close your fist on the cloth for ten seconds. If it comes out badly creased, it will crease on you all day. A little crease in linen is normal.

## Check the colour

Rub a corner with a damp cloth. If colour comes off, ask the shop whether it will run in the wash.

## Buy a little extra

Cloth can shrink the first time it is washed. Ask your tailor whether they wash it first and how much extra they would like you to buy.
