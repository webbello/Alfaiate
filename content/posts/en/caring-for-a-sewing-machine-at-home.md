A sewing machine that is cleaned and covered will outlast one that is not. A few small habits make the difference.

## Brush out the lint

Fluff from the thread gathers around the needle plate and the bobbin case. Switch the machine off, and clear it with a small soft brush after each big project.

## Change the needle

A blunt needle snags the cloth and skips stitches. Change it when you start a new project, or if you hear a soft thud as it goes through.

## Oil only where the manual says

Some machines need a drop of oil in certain places, and some should never be oiled. Follow the manual, and wipe away extra oil before you sew.

## Cover it

Dust is the enemy. A simple cloth cover over the machine keeps it clean between uses.

If the machine still jams, take it to a repair person rather than forcing it.
