Before you take a garment home, turn it inside out and look closely. These five checks take two minutes.

1. **Even stitches.** The stitches in a seam should be the same length, with no loops or gaps.
2. **Straight seams.** Hold the seam up: it should not wander or pucker the cloth.
3. **Tidy insides.** Raw edges should be finished so they do not fray in the wash.
4. **Strong ends.** Where a seam stops, the thread should be fastened with a few stitches back over itself.
5. **Matching patterns.** On stripes and checks, the lines should meet across seams and pockets.

If something is wrong, say so politely before you leave. A good tailor would rather fix it today than hear about it later.
