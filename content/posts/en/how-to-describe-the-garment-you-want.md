Most misunderstandings with a tailor start with a word that means one thing to you and another to them. A few habits help.

## Start with where and when

Say what the garment is for: an office, a wedding, school, everyday wear. The same shirt is cut differently for each.

## Show, then tell

A photo of a garment you like is worth a hundred words. Point out what you like about it: the collar, the length, the way it hangs. Then say what you would change.

## Be clear about fit

Words like “loose” and “fitted” are not the same for everyone. Ask the tailor to pinch the fabric on you, or compare with a garment you own, until you both agree.

## Agree the details

Pockets, lining, buttons and hems all add up. Ask which are included and write the agreement down before work begins.
