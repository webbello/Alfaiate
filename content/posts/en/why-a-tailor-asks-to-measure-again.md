If your tailor asks to measure you again at the next fitting, that is usually good news. It means they are checking their work.

## Bodies change

Weight, posture and even the time of day change your measurements. A few centimetres in the waist can make a trouser feel very different.

## Cloth moves

Cutting and sewing change how fabric hangs. A second look shows where it pulls, gapes or hangs too long.

## Small errors add up

A mistake of half a centimetre in one place is small. In four places it changes how the whole garment sits.

## What you can do

Stand naturally, not stiffly, and breathe normally. Wear the same kind of clothes each time. Tell the tailor honestly if something feels tight or loose, because you are the only one who can feel it.
