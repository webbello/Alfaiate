When a favourite garment no longer fits, you have two choices: have it altered, or have a new one made. A few questions help you decide.

## How much has to change?

Taking in a waist, shortening a hem or sleeve, and replacing a zip are everyday alterations. Changing the shoulders or the whole shape of a jacket is a big job.

## Is there enough cloth?

You can take cloth away much more easily than you can add it. If a garment is too small, ask the tailor whether there is spare cloth inside the seams.

## Is the cloth still good?

A thin or worn fabric may not hold new stitches. If the cloth is tired, a new garment will last longer.

## Ask for a quote on both

A tailor can usually tell you the cost of each in a few minutes. Compare them, and remember that a new garment is made to fit you exactly.
