Formal clothes that are worn a few times a year are often ruined by how they wait, not how they are worn.

## Use a wide hanger

A thin wire hanger pulls the shoulders out of shape. Use a wide, curved hanger for jackets, and clip the skirt or trousers to a hanger of their own.

## Let it breathe

Do not seal clothes in plastic. Use a cotton bag, or an old clean sheet, so air can move around the cloth.

## Clean before you store

Marks you cannot see now, such as sweat or food, can darken and attract moths. Have the garment cleaned, or air it well, before you put it away.

## Keep it dry

Store it away from damp walls and the kitchen. A dry cupboard with a few cedar blocks or dried lavender is much better than a shelf in a humid room.
