You do not need a tailor to sew on a button or close a small seam. You do need a few minutes and a steady hand.

## Sewing on a button

1. Match the thread to the cloth, and use it doubled for strength.
2. Put a matchstick or a pin on top of the button so the stitches stay a little loose.
3. Sew up and down through the holes four or five times.
4. Take the matchstick out, wind the thread around the stalk, and finish with two small stitches under the button.

## Closing a seam

For a seam that has opened, sew small stitches along the old line, a little tighter than the cloth around it. Finish with a knot on the inside.

## When to ask a tailor

Holes in the middle of the cloth, tears in a stretchy fabric, and anything on a jacket lining are best left to a professional. A repair done badly is harder to fix than a repair not done at all.
