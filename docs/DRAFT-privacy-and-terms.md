**What we collect, why, and who sees it**

| When | What | Why | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|---|
| You send a request | Name, phone number (WhatsApp), what you want made, area, optional notes, your consent, a reference code | To pass your request to a tailor and follow up | Us; the tailor we choose, only the details needed to contact you |
| A business or group sends an enquiry | The above plus organisation name and group size | Same | Same |
| A tailor uses "Join us" | Name, phone, business name, area, what they make, optional message, consent | So we can call them about joining | Us only. It does not create a listing |
| You sign up for updates (if switched on) | Email address, consent | To send updates | Us only [and the email service named here] |
| You tap WhatsApp or Call on a tailor | A count per tailor and day | To see which contact methods are used | Us. It is not linked to you |
